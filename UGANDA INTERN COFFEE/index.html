Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026

Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala, Uganda

About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 is a major event organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF). It brings together coffee farmers, exporters, investors, researchers and international coffee industry professionals.

The three-day event will provide opportunities for networking, business partnerships, exhibitions and discussions about innovation, coffee production and the future of Uganda's coffee industry.
